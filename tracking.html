Tracking Code Format

  • Format: Usually starts with "THC" followed by 9-12 digits
  • Example: THC123456789 or THC2025010001
  • Case: Not case-sensitive (THC123 = thc123)
  • Length: Typically 12-15 characters total

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What if my tracking code doesn't work?

If your tracking code isn't working, it may take 24-48 hours to activate after shipping. Contact us if the issue persists.

🕐 How often is tracking information updated?

Tracking information is updated in real-time as your package moves through our delivery network, typically every 2-4 hours.

📦 Can I change my delivery address?

Address changes are possible before shipping. Contact us immediately if you need to modify your delivery address.

🚚 What if I miss my delivery?

Our delivery partners will attempt redelivery or leave your package at a secure location. You'll receive instructions via SMS.
